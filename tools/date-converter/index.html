Calculation method

Dates are calculated with the browser’s Persian, Gregorian and civil Hijri calendars. Civil Hijri is arithmetic and can differ from the official Iranian lunar calendar or moon sightings. Age uses the birth calendar; total days use civil dates, not elapsed 24-hour blocks. No birth time is assumed.

Birthday files add a single all-day event when imported into your calendar app.

How to convert a date

Select the source calendar, enter the year, month and day, and choose Convert date. Persian, Gregorian and calculated civil Hijri results appear together, with the weekday. You can also paste a year-first date such as 1405/09/02.

Age in years, months and days

The age calculator counts completed calendar years and months from your birth date to today or a date you choose. It also shows total days and the next birthday. For a birthday on a leap day, choose the last day of the month or the first day of the following month in non-leap years.

Date difference and the monthly calendar

Compare two dates, add or subtract days, weeks, months or years, and browse the monthly calendar. The calculated Hijri result can differ from moon-sighting calendars.

Calculations run in your browser. Dates are not submitted to a server. A temporary session draft preserves your entries only when you use the language switch on this page.

Analog clock and time zones

The analog hands and digital display show the same time in the zone you choose. Select Tehran, your device zone, UTC or another listed city. Choose the signature, classic or minimal face. The clock follows your device time; it does not contact an atomic time server.
