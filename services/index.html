Choose a useful starting point

An introduction site, a shared management application and an Android app solve different problems. A website may be enough to explain your services and collect inquiries. A team workflow may need a database, access rules and reports. An app may need device integration and its own release process. The service pages explain those differences so you can describe the right scope before committing to a build.
